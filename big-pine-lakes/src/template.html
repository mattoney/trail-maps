🗺 Trail overview ›
Out-and-back along North Fork Big Pine Creek: waterfalls and the Lon Chaney cabin low down, then glacial First–Third Lakes (that milky turquoise is rock flour from Palisade Glacier), with the spur to Fifth Lake near the top. Roughly 7,800–11,200 ft, so pace the altitude. Overnights need an Inyo NF wilderness permit; day hikes don't. No water past the lakes — filter there.
